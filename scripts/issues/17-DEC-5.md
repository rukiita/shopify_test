### Question
When a webhook arrives, should the GraphQL refetch and upsert run synchronously within the request, or be queued for later?

### Options
1. Process synchronously within the request
2. Enqueue a job and return 200 immediately

### Findings
- Shopify webhooks time out after about five seconds https://hookdeck.com/webhooks/platforms/shopify-webhooks-features-and-best-practices-guide
- At this task's scale (a development store with dozens of orders), fetch and upsert should be well under five seconds. To be measured in Step 3
